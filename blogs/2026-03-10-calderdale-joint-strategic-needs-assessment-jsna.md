All Health and Wellbeing boards are responsible for producing a JSNA. They are used to identify the priorities to improve health and wellbeing, and to reduce health inequalities across the borough. Calderdale’s JSNA can be found at Joint strategic needs assessment (JSNA) | Calderdale Council.
